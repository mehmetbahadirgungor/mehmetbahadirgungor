### 01 / Featured product

#### [Athlor](https://athlor.fit/) — AI-powered fitness & nutrition

A mobile product built for **iOS and Android**, bringing personalized training, nutrition tracking, and AI assistance into one experience.

- **Training:** Personalized workout plans, workout tracking, and progress insights.
- **Nutrition:** Food recognition, meal logging, and macro tracking.
- **AI:** Gemini-powered coaching and personalized recommendations.
- **Engineering:** Flutter mobile client; Firebase services; Node.js / TypeScript backend; Python API; Next.js website.

**Tech:** `Flutter` · `GetX` · `Firebase` · `Node.js` · `TypeScript` · `Python` · `Gemini`
